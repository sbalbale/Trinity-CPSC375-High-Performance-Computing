# Strassen

> [!abstract]
> **Volker Strassen** is a German mathematician known for his contributions to the study of algorithms. His most famous achievement in HPC is the discovery of **Strassen's Algorithm**, the first algorithm to multiply matrices in sub-$O(n^3)$ time.

## Key Contributions

- **Strassen's Algorithm (1969)**: Proved that $O(n^3)$ was not the theoretical lower bound for matrix multiplication. His $O(n^{2.81})$ approach changed the field of computational complexity.
- **Complexity Theory**: His work laid the foundation for modern linear algebra optimization.
