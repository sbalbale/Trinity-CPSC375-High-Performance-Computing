# Gene Amdahl

> [!abstract]
> **Gene Amdahl** (1922–2015) was a computer architect and high-tech entrepreneur, best known for his work on IBM mainframes and for formulating **Amdahl's Law**, which defines the fundamental limits of parallel speedup.

## Key Contributions
- **Amdahl's Law**: Proposed in 1967, it argues that the speedup of a program is limited by its sequential fraction.
- **IBM System/360**: Chief architect of the legendary mainframe series.

## Connections
* **Associated Concept:** [[amdahls-law]].
* **Field of Study:** Parallel computing, computer architecture.
