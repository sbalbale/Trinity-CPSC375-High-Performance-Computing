#### Head of the Riverfront — Course Notes
- Launches from Riverside Park; start line ~3 miles downstream.
- Upstream direction — tidal current works against you; power output will be higher than split suggests.
- Three bridges to navigate: **Charter Oak Bridge**, **Founders Bridge**, **Bulkeley Bridge** — steering is a training variable.
- Stroke rate: 24–28 SPM. Volume training emphasis Sept–Oct.

#### NESCAC — Finals Overlap Protocol
Finals end May 8. Race is May 10. **48-hour window is the taper and recovery period simultaneously.**
- May 4–8: Zero HIIT. Zone 2 only. Sleep ≥9h. No new lifts.
- May 8 (eve): Full carb load. Familiar meal. Early sleep.
- May 9: Light Z1/Z2 30 min + paddle. Dynamic warm-up drill. Bed by 22:00.
- May 10 (race day): 3h pre-race carb meal (60–80g, low fat, zero experiment). 20 min dynamic warm-up.

## CRASH-B Prep Block (6 Weeks, February)

Concept2 erg race: 2,000m. **Drag factor must be verified at 120–130 before any test piece.**
Dust accumulation in the flywheel cage alters resistance — use the PM5 monitor to confirm drag factor directly, not damper position.

Competitive benchmark: sub-6:30 (strong collegiate HW). Elite: sub-6:10.
Sustained power required: >400W for competitive splits.

```
Week 1: Base flush — 5× Z2 60min, 2× Z3 30min
Week 2: Introduce threshold — 3× 8min Z4, 2× Z2 60min
Week 3: 4×2k at goal pace +5sec, full recovery between
Week 4: 3×2k at goal pace +3sec + 6k simulation piece
Week 5: Taper — 2×1k at race pace, volume –50%
Week 6: Race week — short sharp pieces, rest, CRASH-B
```

## Taper Protocol Template (2k Sprint Race)

### Week -2 (2 weeks out)
- Volume –20%. Maintain intensity — Z4/Z5 pieces but shorter.
- Lift: maintenance only. No new max loads.

### Week -1 (Race week)
- Volume –40–50%.
- 2–3 race-sharpening pieces: 4×500m at goal race pace.
- Legs completely clear by Thursday.
- Nutrition: carb load Thursday–Saturday.
- Sleep: non-negotiable ≥9h nightly.

### Race Day
- Light Z1/Z2 warm-up 30 min in the AM.
- Dynamic warm-up 20 min pre-race.
- Pre-race meal 2–3h before: 60–80g carbs, low fat, proven food only.

## Head Race Prep (Fall — 3,800m+ at 24–28 SPM)

Training emphasis: aerobic volume, not intensity. Lactate-at-pace work, not VO₂max.

Key sessions:
- 3× per week: 60–90 min continuous Z2 on the water or erg
- 1× per week: 3×12 min at Z3 (tempo), 5 min rest
- 1× per week: technical paddle with video review (stroke consistency at 24 SPM)
- Bridge navigation: practice steering through the Charter Oak, Founders, and Bulkeley bridges in pairs/fours before racing an eight through them

Power note: upstream racing against tidal current inflates cardiovascular cost. Add 5–8% to session TSS vs. flat water equivalent.
